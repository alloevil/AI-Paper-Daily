### 1. ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **OpenClaw and Ollama in Agentic AI: Toward Fully Autonomous and Scalable AI Agent Systems** (cs.AI)
- 作者：Konstantinos I. Roumeliotis, Ranjan Sapkota
- 关键词：LLM, agent, reasoning, tool use, autonomous, memory, planning
- 摘要：The rapid transition from reactive large language models (LLMs) to persistent, action-capable systems has exposed critical gaps in the architectural understanding of Agentic AI, particularly in separating inference, orchestration, and execution layers for autonomous AI agents. This paper presents a comprehensive, layered architecture for Agentic AI, analyzing OpenClaw and Ollama as a full-stack Agentic AI system.
- 链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28629

### 3. ⭐⭐ **How Hard Does It Think? Analyzing Step-Aware Reasoning Energy in LLM Chain-of-Thought Trajectories** (cs.AI, cs.CL, cs.LG)
- 作者：Hui Wei, Junda Wu, Sheldon Yu
- 关键词：LLM, reasoning
- 摘要：Understanding how computational effort is allocated across individual chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning steps remains an open challenge. We propose Step-Aware Reasoning Energy (SARE), a geometric framework that quantifies effort at the granularity of individual CoT steps.
- 链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28674

### 4. ⭐⭐ **Learning Stateful Predictive Knowledge From Experience** (cs.CL, cs.LG)
- 作者：Yan Song, Xidong Feng, Bo Liu
- 关键词：LLM, agent
- 摘要：As large language model (LLM) agents increasingly learn from experience, they primarily rely on trajectory-level reflection to extract insights. We propose Stateful Knowledge Learning (SKL), shifting the agent's focus from trajectory-level summarization to maintaining Stateful Knowledge.
- 链接：https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28638

### 5. ⭐⭐ **TokenSwap: Benchmarking and Reducing the Modality Gap in Multimodal LLMs** (cs.CL, cs.CV)
- 作者：Andong Hua, Colton Bishop, Igor Mordatch
- 关键词：LLM, multimodal
- 摘要：Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) should generate consistent responses given semantically equivalent inputs across modalities. We introduce TokenSwap, a method that constructs such inputs by replacing textual concepts with semantically aligned images.
